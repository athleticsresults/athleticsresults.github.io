1st Lugano Trophy Lugano 1961 - Qualifying Rounds

From 1961 to 1985 the competition was held in two phases. Nations were grouped by geographical zone; the winning team in each zone qualified for the final. Three athletes scored in each of the 20km and 50km races. Individual results are listed on the 20km and 50km pages.
