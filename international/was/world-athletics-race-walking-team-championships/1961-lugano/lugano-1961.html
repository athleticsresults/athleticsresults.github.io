1Four nations contested the final. Ten nations took part in the qualifying rounds, with zone winners advancing. The Federal Republic of Germany and the German Democratic Republic competed as a combined German team in qualifying. Turkey did not start in Zone 4, so Hungary advanced by walkover.

The event was staged as the Lugano Trophy, the first edition of what is now the World Athletics Race Walking Team Championships. The men's team ranking combined the 20km and 50km races. Great Britain & NI and Sweden tied on 53 points; the trophy was awarded to Great Britain & NI as the higher-placed of the two at 50km.
